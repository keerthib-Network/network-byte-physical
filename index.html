How Ethernet detects corruption

Conceptually, the sender calculates an FCS/CRC value, the receiver calculates its own value, and the values are compared.

Healthy frame
Received FCS: 101101
Calculated FCS: 101101
Frame accepted
Corrupted frame
Received FCS: 101101
Calculated FCS: 101001
Frame rejected

Conceptual FCS/CRC demonstration—not a standards-complete CRC implementation. CRC/FCS errors can point toward cabling, interference, optics, or related link problems.
